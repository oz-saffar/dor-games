# Audio Assets for Dor's Games

## Required Audio Files (Hebrew)

Place the following MP3 files in this directory:

### Feedback Sounds:
- `kol_hakavod.mp3` - Voice saying "כל הכבוד!" (Great job!)
- `hatzlachta.mp3` - Voice saying "הצלחת!" (You succeeded!)
- `tamshich_lenasot.mp3` - Voice saying "תמשיך לנסות! אתה יכול!" (Keep trying! You can do it!)

### Background Music:
- `background_music.mp3` - Soft instrumental acoustic guitar music (outdoor/playing vibe)

## Notes:
- All voice recordings should be in Hebrew
- Keep audio files short (1-3 seconds for feedback)
- Background music should be gentle and non-intrusive
- Volume will be controlled programmatically
